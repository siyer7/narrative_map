* Consider what a spike in the sparse memory nodes raster (2_surprisal_vs_discontinuity.md) actually names. A switch never moves one narr's match alone, so every spike is a *set* of narrs moving together. Call that set a composition, and ask how many compositions there can be and which ones a story actually visits.
* Say the narrs and their chain are as in stage 2: narr1, narr2, narr3, a markov chain over the sets of narrs that can be on together, never silent, p(stay on), p(stay off) = .95, .8 for narr1 and .93, .93 for narr2 and narr3, min run 4, narr1 starting on, seed 3.
* Say a composition can be read off which narrs are on, without the embedding. A narr's match is 0 while it is off and 1/sqrt(n_narrs_on) while it is on, so the match moves when that narr switches, and also when it merely holds while the count changes. The composition at a timestep is the set of narrs whose match moved.
* Say the number of possible compositions is num_compositional_narrs = 2**n_narrs - 1 - n_narrs. Of the 2**n_narrs subsets of the narrs, the empty one is not a composition (it is a timestep where nothing switched) and the n_narrs singletons cannot occur (a switch changes the count for everyone still on), leaving the subsets of size 2 or more. The script asserts both: every composition has more than one narr, and every composition seen is one of the enumerated subsets.
* Say the left panel plots that against the number of narratives from 2 to 5, against the number of bayesian surprisal events the same chain produces over 100 timesteps -- an event being a timestep where any narr switches on, so narrs arriving together count once, averaged over 20 seeds and drawn with the standard error of that mean. The compositional events go 1, 4, 11, 26 while the bayesian surprisal events go 4.6, 8.4, 11.2, 13.6 (sd about 1.8, standard error about 0.4), and the two cross at 4 narratives. That crossing is the compositional advantage: past it there are more distinct compositions available than there are surprisal events for a single global signal to spend.
* Say the right panel is a cube, one axis per narr, each axis 0 or 1 for whether that narr is in the composition. Its 2**n_narrs corners are then every subset of the narrs, and the formula is the geometry: the origin (0,0,0) is the timesteps where nothing switched and its three neighbours are the singletons a switch cannot produce, so none of those four is drawn at all. The 4 corners at least 2 edges out are the compositions, and each carries one jittered circle per occurrence, on a faint stem back to its corner. An arrow runs out along each narr axis, named at its tip in that narr colour.
* Say seed 3 gives 12 compositions over 100 timesteps, visiting 3 of the 4 possible: narr1 & narr2 6 times, narr1 & narr3 4 times, all three twice, and narr2 & narr3 never. The script prints all four counts.

## Notes

* the crossing is not resolved. At 4 narratives the two are 11 against 11.2, a gap of half a standard error, so the crossing point is only placed to within about one narrative. It is the direction that is solid -- one line is exponential and the other nearly flat -- not the exact narrative count where they meet.
* the two lines are not the same kind of count, and the crossing is not intrinsic. The compositional events are how many are *possible*, a combinatorial number that depends only on the number of narratives. The bayesian surprisal events are how many *occurred*, averaged over seeds but still growing with n_time, so a longer story lifts the blue line and pushes the crossing right. Read the panel as "possible slots against events to fill them at this story length", not as two curves of the same type.

* the cube says which compositions are possible, not which the story visits. Seed 3 visits only 3 of the 4, missing (0,1,1), and the counts are printed rather than drawn. That miss is narr1's persistence showing through: it is on for 90 of 100 timesteps, so it is almost always the bystander a switch drags along, and the composition without it is the one that never occurs.
* a corner says which narrs moved but not which one switched and which were dragged. Both are in the set, and the set is all a spike carries. Recovering the switcher needs the magnitude back, i.e. reading the match as a projection rather than a cosine.
* num_compositional_narrs grows like 2**n_narrs while the events it has to label grow far slower (4.6 to 13.7 over the same range), so most corners go unvisited as narrs are added. Here 1 of 4 corners is never reached in 100 timesteps, and at 5 narratives there are 26 corners for about 14 events.
